FAQ

Website Design FAQ: Cost, Timeline & SEO

Straight answers to the questions business owners ask before getting a new website, from pricing and launch time to Google rankings and what happens after launch.

Our plans start at $59/month with a $0 setup fee and a free mockup during our current limited-time offer. Regular pricing is a one-time build fee starting at $999 plus the monthly plan. Every plan includes hosting, security, and monthly edits, so there are no hidden fees. See full details in our pricing section above.

The benefit of working with us is that most sites launch fast. A Starter site takes about 3-5 days from your discovery call, a Business site 5-7 days, and a Growth & Ecommerce build 1-2 weeks. Timelines depend on how quickly you can share content and feedback, and we give you a firm timeline before any work starts.

Think of it as a care plan for your site: hosting, security updates, backups, uptime monitoring, and a set amount of edits every month (30 minutes on Starter, 1 hour on Business, 1.5 hours on Growth & Ecommerce). We handle the technical side so you don't have to. Just call or email when something needs to change.

If customers look for businesses like yours online, yes. A website is the one place you fully control: it shows what you offer, builds trust before the first call, and gives Google something to rank. Social media pages and directory listings help, but they can't take bookings, sell products, or show up in search the way your own site can.

Your site is hand-built around your business, not dropped into a template or a page builder. That means faster load times, cleaner code for search engines, and a design that doesn't look like your competitors'. You also get a real person to call when you need help, not a support queue.

Local SEO helps your business appear when people search for services near them, including Google Maps results and searches like "plumber near me." If you serve customers in a specific area, you need it. Every plan includes local SEO and Google Business Profile setup.

New sites usually need a few months to build momentum in search, depending on your industry and competition. Your Google Business Profile and local listings can start helping sooner. SEO is steady, ongoing work, which is why our higher plans include regular tuning and reporting.
